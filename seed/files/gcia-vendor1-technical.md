# Technical Envelope — Marine Jetty Rehabilitation
Bidder: Qamar Arcwave Systems W.L.L. (fictitious)

## 1. Method statement
Pile condition survey by divers and ultrasonic thickness testing; jacket repair with marine-grade epoxy grout; precast deck slabs on berths 3 to 5 installed from a jack-up barge to keep two berths operational.

## 2. Programme
13 months from site handover, with berth 4 closed for no more than 10 weeks.

## 3. Experience
Three comparable jetty rehabilitations completed in the last 6 years (references attached).

## 4. Documents enclosed
Commercial registration, bid bond (2%), HSE plan, programme of works.
