# Kuwait Energy Authority (fictitious) — Request for Proposal KEA-2026-088
# IT Service Desk Platform

All organisations, people and figures in this document are fictitious and used for demonstration only.

## 1. Scope of work
1.1 The Authority seeks a vendor to supply, implement and support an IT service desk platform for approximately 1,200 employees across the head office and four field sites.
1.2 The scope covers incident, request, problem, change and knowledge management, a self-service portal in Arabic and English, and a 3-year support and maintenance period.
1.3 Data migration of approximately 85,000 historical tickets from the current system is included.

## 2. Technical requirements
2.1 The platform shall be deployed on-premises in the Authority data centre; no customer data may leave Kuwait.
2.2 The self-service portal shall support Arabic (right-to-left) and English, switchable per user.
2.3 The platform shall integrate with the Authority directory service for single sign-on.
2.4 The platform shall provide an audit trail of every ticket change, retained for at least 7 years.
2.5 Availability target: 99.5% during working hours (07:00–15:00 Kuwait time, Sunday–Thursday).
2.6 The vendor shall provide at least two certified engineers on site during the first 3 months after go-live.

## 3. Required documents
Each bid must include all of the following; bids missing any item may be disqualified at the committee's discretion.
3.1 Commercial registration certificate (valid).
3.2 Bid bond equal to 2% of the bid value, valid for 90 days after the submission deadline.
3.3 ISO/IEC 27001 certificate of the vendor or of the proposed hosting/support organisation.
3.4 Technical proposal addressing every requirement in section 2.
3.5 Financial proposal with a priced bill of quantities (licences, implementation, 3-year support), in KWD.

## 4. Evaluation criteria
4.1 Technical solution — 50%.
4.2 Experience and team — 20%.
4.3 Price — 30%.
4.4 Only bids that are administratively compliant (section 3) proceed to technical and financial evaluation.

## 5. Timeline
5.1 Clarification questions accepted until 5 working days before the submission deadline.
5.2 Submission deadline as published on the TenderFlow portal; late bids are not accepted.
5.3 Bid opening by the tender committee immediately after the deadline.
5.4 Expected award within 30 days of opening; implementation within 6 months of award.
