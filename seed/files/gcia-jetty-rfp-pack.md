# Request for Proposal — Marine Jetty Rehabilitation (GCIA, fictitious)

## 1. Scope
Rehabilitation of the 240 m service jetty at the Northern Harbour: pile inspection and repair, deck slab replacement on berths 3 to 5, cathodic protection, fenders and lighting. Works must be completed within 14 months of the site handover.

## 2. Eligibility
Bidders must hold the Ministry of Commerce activity code ENG-02 (Marine works) or ENG-01 (Civil works) at Second grade or better. Tender documents must be purchased before a bid is submitted.

## 3. Two-envelope procedure
3.1 The technical envelope and the financial envelope are submitted together and stay sealed until the deadline.
3.2 Technical envelopes are opened first and scored on the technical and experience criteria. The pass mark is 70 out of 100.
3.3 Only bids that pass have their financial envelope opened. The financial envelopes of failed bids stay sealed.

## 4. Required documents
Commercial registration, bid bond of 2% of the bid value, method statement, programme of works, HSE plan, priced bill of quantities.

## 5. Evaluation criteria
Technical solution and method statement (50), experience on comparable marine works (20), price (30).

## 6. Deadlines
Clarification questions close 5 days before the submission deadline. Bids remain valid for 120 days.
