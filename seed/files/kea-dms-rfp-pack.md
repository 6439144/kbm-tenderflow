# Kuwait Energy Authority (fictitious) — Request for Proposal KEA-2026-102
# Document Management System

All organisations, people and figures in this document are fictitious and used for demonstration only.

## 1. Scope of work
1.1 Supply and implementation of an enterprise document management system (DMS) for approximately 900 users.
1.2 Scanning and Arabic/English OCR of approximately 1.4 million legacy pages held in the central archive.
1.3 Records retention schedules and legal hold for contracts, correspondence and HR files.

## 2. Technical requirements
2.1 The DMS shall be deployed on-premises in the Authority data centre. Private or public cloud hosting is not accepted.
2.2 Full Arabic right-to-left user interface and Arabic full-text search.
2.3 Integration with the Authority correspondence system and directory service.
2.4 Document versioning, check-in/check-out and role-based access at folder and document level.
2.5 Every access, download and change shall be recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

## 3. Required documents
3.1 Commercial registration certificate.
3.2 Bid bond equal to 2% of the bid value.
3.3 ISO/IEC 27001 certificate.
3.4 Technical proposal.
3.5 Financial proposal (priced bill of quantities in KWD).

## 4. Evaluation criteria
4.1 Technical solution — 50%. 4.2 Experience and team — 20%. 4.3 Price — 30%.

## 5. Timeline
5.1 Submission deadline as published on the TenderFlow portal.
5.2 Implementation within 9 months of award, including OCR of the legacy archive.
